BA FAQ
❓ FAQ
1. How can I submit my complaint? ⌄
To submit a report, go to the Submit Report page and enter the required details, upload a photo if available, and click Submit Report. You will receive a Report ID that can be used to track your report's status.
2. How can I track my report? ⌄
Visit the Track Report page and enter your Report ID in the search field. The system will display the current status of your report, such as Pending, In Progress, or Resolved.
3. What types of issues can I report? ⌄
Residents may report community concerns such as damaged roads, streetlight problems, drainage issues, garbage collection concerns, public safety issues, and other matters that require barangay attention.
